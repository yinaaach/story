# The Eldritch Dossier: Aberration Alerts in Existence

Author: anonymous registered nurse | Location: Jurisdiction of a Taichung City Police Station

**🚨 Report Subject:** Collective Bizarre Behavior within the Jurisdiction of a Taichung City Police Station
**⚠ Report Level:** Core Anomaly (High Risk of Mental Contamination)

**I. Case Overview**
    This case was initially logged in July of Year 116 by a resident of Taichung City (hereinafter referred to as "the Reporter"). 
    The Reporter claims that over the dozen or so years of their residency, the outcomes of legal cases unfolding in their surroundings have consistently failed to align with factual elements...

**II. Investigator's Field Observation Log (Excerpt)**
    July 15, Year 116, 01:24 AM. 
    Tossing and turning... I simply cannot sleep. The events unfolding in this house defy all human reason...
    I stand before the mirror—its surface slightly fogged and cloudy. When I deliberately raise my right hand to check my watch, I notice that my reflection inexplicably projects a haggard, decayed state, a face visibly aged beyond my actual years. It is no illusion. The person in the mirror has sagging facial skin and a frail, powerless lower body. But I feel no acute underlying illness; something is inexplicably amiss—a sensation I cannot quite put into words.
    What truly chills me to the bone is that the children have also begun showing anomalies, one after another. The eldest child at times stares blankly into space, his movements sluggish, entirely oblivious to his own condition. His school grades have plummeted, and he has become severely lethargic during the day; yet past midnight, he remains wide awake, spiraling into a state of hyperactivity, restlessness, and sudden bursts of irritability.
    As for the grandfather, his condition is even more bizarre. He consumes massive amounts of food, yet his body remains unnaturally emaciated. And when he sleeps, his breathing ceases for prolonged, agonizing intervals, before he abruptly gasps for air.

**III. Clinical Anomalies & Collective Apathy**
    The grandfather occasionally reports somatic discomfort and acid reflux. Tophi are prominently visible on his limb joints, and he has recently presented with a primary complaint of severe dysphagia. Compounding our situation, the other child residing with us has begun demonstrating an inability to focus their right eye. As a registered nurse, my professional training allows me to recognize exactly where the pathology lies at a single glance. Observation of the cohabitants' dietary habits reveals a daily intake consisting strictly of sweets, alcohol, spicy foods, scalding water, pickled items, and hyper-sodium provisions. But what can be done? These traditional lifestyle patterns are too deeply entrenched to alter. Moreover, because they lack any medical background, they suffer from **profound anosognosia**—utterly blind to their own morbid decay.
    I tried to communicate, only to find that it was entirely futile. The child's father snapped at me, "Everyone eats like this, and everyone is perfectly fine. What is wrong with you? Are you out of your mind?" When I turned to the grandmother, she fired back in anger, "You say we can't eat this, we can't eat that—then what on earth are we supposed to eat?!" Finally, I spoke to the grandfather, who simply pulled traditional family rank on me, "Just listen to your grandmother. You are the junior here; why do you have so many opinions? Are you having a mental breakdown?" This collective apathy and incomprehension has even begun to metastasize outward, spreading to the police, social workers, and the courts.

**IV. Institutional Failure & Systematic Gaslighting**
    Every single morning at 5:00 AM, the grandfather and grandmother deliberately come to my house just to ring the doorbell. When I went to report this to the police, thoroughly exhausted and pushed to my absolute limit, the officer simply said, "There is nothing the police can do about someone ringing your doorbell at 5:00 AM every day. Besides, how do I even know what you're saying is true or false?!" When I explained the situation to the social worker, she merely evaded my gaze and brushed me off, saying, "The doctor said you have paranoia. Look... isn't the grandfather standing right there in front of you, perfectly fine? Frankly, I'm not a medical professional, so I don't really understand what you're talking about." Throughout this entire period, I have been continuously pressured by everyone around me to visit a psychiatric clinic. Yet all the while, the children are being relentlessly forced to consume items loaded with high sugar, high sodium, and foods laced with alcohol—and the police and social worker choose to completely blind themselves to all of it.
    What drove me into utter despair was what happened when I told the Judge about this case in the courthouse. I watched with my own eyes as my family members—who are Hepatitis B carriers and the other already exhibiting clear symptoms of esophageal cancer—were continuously induced by other relatives to drink alcohol, forced to persist in this fatal lifestyle. In a desperate attempt to halt this impending tragedy, I resorted to an extreme method of warning: I splashed water-based paint onto their vehicle. It was a completely safe act; the paint could be easily washed away with water without causing any permanent damage to the car. Yet, because I had aggressively tried to stop their lethal dietary behaviors, I was convicted of Domestic Violence. And because I had splashed that water-based paint, I was convicted of Criminal Destruction of Property.
    Throughout the judicial proceedings, every single person I came into contact with seemed utterly incapable of comprehending the stark reality of my words. What I fail to understand is that some of them are university graduates—is this truly what passes for a normal level of baseline knowledge? I am constantly breaking out in cold sweats, unable to sleep. I question the doctors' professionalism.

**V. The Warped Reality of Expertise**
    Because the eldest child has reached 10 years of age, approximately one-quarter of the population will lose their immunity from the Hepatitis B vaccine by this point. Although antibodies might still persist in the system, from a professional medical standpoint, a booster shot is highly recommended. Yet, when I communicated this to the school teacher, pointing out that the school's physical examination forms lacked a crucial warning—“cohabitants of Hepatitis B carriers are advised to receive a booster shot if their antibody test returns negative”—the teacher simply replied: "I'm not a professional, I don't understand what you're talking about. Besides, it costs money." Driven by sheer helplessness, I had no choice but to personally take the child to the Family Medicine department at the hospital for a booster. To my absolute disbelief, the attending physician on-site actually said, "At this age, they need to inject three full shots! It only works if they inject all three!"
    But I remembered vividly from my textbook during my studies that at this age, only a single booster injection is required, and that the number of doses increases only with advanced age. The more I thought about it, the more localized reality felt warped. I walked over to the counter to explain the rationale to the administrative staff, only for them to reply in a matter-of-fact tone, "Whatever the doctor says is right!" Suppressing my rising agitation, I said, "Could you please direct me to the Chief of the Medical Department? I would like to discuss this with them." Ultimately, the Chief of the Medical Department stated, "You are entirely correct. Indeed, only one dose is required. I will ensure the physicians undergo re-education..."

**VI. The Ultimate Conclusion**
    And so, I was left to watch helplessly as the grandfather's condition deteriorated, while the biological father relentlessly guided the children to consume improper food. Ultimately, the very tragedy I feared most struck—due to the prolonged improper diet, the child suffered abnormal cerebral electrical discharges and was hospitalized after an epileptic seizure!! As I stood by the hospital bed, scared completely out of my wits and staring my child, the physician merely dismissed it lightheartedly, saying, "It's just benign epilepsy." Even with things having reached this breaking point, everyone around me continues to treat me like a lunatic, constantly pressuring me to visit a psychiatric clinic... Looking at my sick child, and looking at this profoundly cold world where no one understands medical expertise, and where everyone remains entirely indifferent... I truly, honestly, do not know what to do anymore......
    In the end... the news I dreaded most became reality: the grandfather was diagnosed with esophageal cancer. It was discovered too late—he was already in stage four (the advanced stage), where the five-year survival rate is typically less than 5% to 10%. He eventually passed away. A social worker called to apologize, and the child's father also expressed remorse... Do you think they genuinely cared about this relative? Or perhaps there was a hidden motive of self-interest at play all along? Or could it be... simply the ultimate conclusion of a slow-moving murder carried out collectively?????????

# 詭異生活異常通報事件簿

作者：匿名護理師 | 地點：臺中市某派出所屬轄

**🚨 通報主題：** 臺中市某派出所所轄集體詭異行為
**⚠ 通報級別：** 核心異常（高度精神污染風險）

**一、 事件概述**
    本案由居住於臺中市居民（以下簡稱通報人）於 116 年 7 月份首次提報。
    通報人宣稱其所居住之十幾年，生活周遭發生司法案件結果總是不符合事實要件......

**二、 調查員實地觀測紀錄（節錄自調查日誌）**
    116 年 7 月 15 日，凌晨 01:24。
    翻來覆去......總是睡不著。這個屋子裡發生的事情，總是違反了人類的常理......

**三、 臨床異常與集體冷漠**
    阿公有時表示自己身體不舒服、胃食道逆流，四肢關節也可以明顯看到痛風石，近期甚至還出現了難以吞嚥的主訴。更糟糕的是，同住家人的另一位孩子，右眼竟然開始無法聚焦。我是護理師，專業訓練讓我這一眼就知道問題出在哪裡。觀察同住者飲食習慣，天天都是甜食、酒精、辣、熱開水、醃製物與高鈉食物。但這能怎麼辦？傳統的生活習慣早就根深體固，難以變更，而且他們都不是醫療人員，根本沒有病識感。
我試著去溝通，卻發現根本沒有用。孩子的父親對我說：「大家都是這樣的飲食習慣，大家都沒事，妳哪裡有問題？是神經病嗎？」去跟阿嬤說，阿嬤卻生氣地回：「妳這不能吃、那不能吃，到底可以吃什麼？？？」接著跟阿公說，阿公也是提出長輩的架子：「就聽阿嬤的，妳是晚輩，怎麼這麼多意見？妳是不是精神有問題。」這種集體的漠視與不理解，甚至向外擴散到了警察、社工和法院。
**四、 制度性失敗與系統性煤氣燈效應**
    每天清晨五點，阿公阿嬤故意到我家按門鈴。當我心力交瘁地去報案，警察卻說：「每天有人清晨五點到妳家按門鈴，警察沒辦法處理。我怎麼知道妳說的是真的假的！！」去跟社工說明情況，社工也只是眼神閃爍地敷衍我：「醫生說妳有妄想症，妳看......阿公不是還好好的站在妳面前嗎？其實我不是醫療人員，所以妳說的話我聽不懂。」在這段期間，我一直被周圍的人要求去看身心科。但與此同時，孩子卻一直被迫進食高糖、高鈉、甚至還加入了酒的料理，警察和社工卻完全忽視這一切。
    更讓我絕望的是到了法院。因為我看著身邊有 B 肝帶原和已經明顯出現了食道癌症狀的家人，卻還是一直被其他親屬引誘飲酒，強迫這樣生活。為了企圖制止這場悲劇發生，我在絕望中採取了極端的警告手段——我在對方的車上潑灑了水性漆。這明明是安全的、只要用水清洗就能恢復、完全不會毀損車輛的作法，卻因為強力制止這些飲食行為，我被判了家庭暴力；又因為潑了水性漆，被判了毀損罪。
在審理案件的過程中，那些接洽的人每一個都聽不懂我說的實情。但讓我無法理解的是，他們有的是大學畢業，這難道是正常的知識水平嗎？我一直在冒汗，睡不好，連外面的醫生也是「普兩光」......?
**五、 專業的扭曲現實**
    因為大孩子 10 歲了，B 肝疫苗免疫力會有四分之一的人群消失。雖然體內可能還是有抗體，但站在醫療專業上，還是建議補打。可是當我跟學校老師溝通，學校體檢表沒有警示，「家中有 B 肝帶原者的同住者在抗體檢驗陰性時，建議補打」，老師卻回我：「我不是專業人員，我聽不懂，而且要花錢。」無奈之下，我只好親自帶孩子到醫院家醫科補打。沒想到現場的醫生竟然說：「這年紀要補打 3 支喔！要打 3 支才有效！」
可是我記得我以前讀書，教科書上明明說打一支就好，年齡愈大、才要補打的支數愈多。我越想越不對，走到櫃檯找行政人員說明原委，對方卻用理所當然的語氣說：「醫生怎麼說，就是對的啊！」我壓著情緒說：「可以請妳幫我找醫療科主任嗎？我跟主任討論。」最後醫療科主任表示：「妳說的沒錯，確實打一支就好，我會對底下的醫生進行再教育……」
**六、 最終結局**
    就這樣，我一邊看著阿公的病情持續惡化，另一邊小孩又持續被生父引導進食不當的食物。最終，最讓人擔心的悲劇還是發生了——孩子因為長期不當飲食，導致大腦異常放電，癲癇發作住院！！當我嚇得魂飛魄散，在病床旁看著孩子時，醫生卻只是輕描淡寫地說：「良性癲癇。」事情到了這個地步，我還是一直被周圍的人當成神經病，一直被逼著要去看身心科……看著生病的孩子，看著這個沒有人聽得懂專業、所有人都異常冷漠的世界，我真的不知道該怎麼辦......
    最終……我最害怕的消息變成了現實：祖父被確診患有食道癌。發現時為時已晚——病情已處於第四期（晚期），而這一階段的五年存活率通常不足 5% 到 10%。他最後還是去世了。一位社工打電話致歉，孩子的父親也表達了悔意… 你認為他們是真心關心這位親人嗎？還是說，曾經有或者背後隱藏著利益的動機？還是說......這只是一場緩慢進行的集體謀殺的最終結論?????????
